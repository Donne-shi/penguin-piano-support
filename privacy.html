起初钢琴 · 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

一句话

起初钢琴不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人信息。

麦克风

App 用麦克风听孩子在钢琴上弹的音，判断有没有弹对。声音只在设备上实时分析，不录音、不保存、不上传。

学习记录

星星、进度、练习时长只保存在这台设备上，用来显示学习报告。我们看不到这些数据。删除 App 就会一起删除。

没有账号、广告和统计

不需要注册账号。App 里没有任何广告，也没有使用任何第三方统计、分析或追踪工具。

购买

购买通过苹果 App Store 完成。付款信息由苹果处理，我们收不到你的姓名、邮箱或付款信息。

儿童

App 为 3–6 岁儿童设计，遵守苹果「儿童」类别的要求。购买和设置都放在家长门（按住 3 秒）后面。

Penguin Piano: Kids Hymns · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short

Penguin Piano: Kids Hymns does not collect, upload or share any personal information.

Microphone

The app uses the microphone to hear the notes your child plays on the piano. Sound is analyzed on the device in real time and is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

Learning progress

Stars, progress and practice time are stored only on this device to show the learning report. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

No accounts, ads or analytics

No sign-up is needed. There are no ads and no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising tools.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email or payment details.

Children

The app is designed for children aged 3–6 and follows Apple's Kids Category guidelines. Purchases and settings are behind a parental gate (press and hold for 3 seconds).
