Common questions

The penguin can't hear my piano.
Allow microphone access in Settings › Penguin Piano. Put the iPad or iPhone on the music stand, close to the keys, in a quiet room. In the app, open the Parent Center › Settings and choose "Set up piano and microphone again".

My piano sounds a little high or low.
Run the microphone setup again from the Parent Center. The app offers to adjust to your piano's tuning.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the Parent Center › Purchase and tap "Restore Purchase". It works on any iPad or iPhone signed in with the same Apple Account, and with Family Sharing.

Can my child skip a level?
Yes. Press and hold "Hold to skip" at the top of a level for 2 seconds, then confirm.

Does the app record my child?
No. The microphone is only used live to hear piano notes. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded, and the app collects no data.

常见问题

企鹅恩恩听不到钢琴声。
请在「设置 › 起初钢琴」里允许使用麦克风。把 iPad 或 iPhone 放在谱架上、靠近琴键，房间尽量安静。在 App 里打开「家长中心 › 设置」，点「重新设置钢琴和麦克风」。

我家的钢琴音有点高或有点低。
在家长中心重新做一次麦克风设置，App 会提示按你家钢琴的音高来听。

怎么恢复购买？
打开「家长中心 › 购买」，点「恢复购买」。同一个 Apple 账号的 iPad 和 iPhone 都可以恢复，也支持家人共享。

孩子可以跳过某一关吗？
可以。在关卡上方按住「按住跳过」2 秒，再点确认。

App 会录下孩子的声音吗？
不会。麦克风只用来实时听钢琴的音，不录音、不保存、不上传，App 不收集任何数据。
